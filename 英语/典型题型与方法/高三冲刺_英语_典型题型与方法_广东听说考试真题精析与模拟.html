广东听说考试真题精析与模拟

⚠️待强化★★★☆☆#高三冲刺#广东听说来源：2024 广东高考英语听说考试真题（Test C / Test B 回忆版）· 广东省教育考试院听说考试说明
广东考情：高频（广东听说每年必考，三题型 Part A/B/C 固定）· 中档 · 分值占英语总分 13.3%（折算后 20 分）· 真题锚定：2024 广东听说 Part C《Tom 走错球场》、Part B 家乡变化访谈、Part A 海洋潮汐能

核心内容

本质理解：广东听说考试（CELST）为人机对话，分 Part A 模仿朗读、Part B 角色扮演、Part C 故事复述三块。本卡用真题母题串讲三题型实操，配套"广东听说考试Part C故事复述技巧"卡的笔记法与复述策略。

一、三题型全景与提分权重

题型任务核心能力占比
Part A 模仿朗读看文听音，跟读模仿语音语调、停顿连贯约 20%
Part B 角色扮演看提示→三问（英译中提问）→五答（中译英回答）听力抓取+口头转述约 27%
Part C 故事复述听两遍故事+梗概关键词→用自己的话复述听记+组织+流利表达约 53%（满分 24/60）

关键信息：Part C 是"性价比最高"的提分项，系统训练可从 12 分提到 20 分以上；内容完整度（8–12 分）权重最高，语言/流利/语音各 3–5 分。

二、真题母题·Part C（2024 广东听说 Test C《Tom 走错球场》）

梗概关键词：Tom, soccer fan, ticket, wrong stadium, teacher Mary, heavy bag, gave a ride, watched together

原文故事（回忆版）：

Version 2: Tom, a devoted soccer enthusiast, had always wanted to experience a live soccer game. Last week, his friend surprised him with a ticket to see his favorite team, making Tom extremely happy. On the day of the game, he left home earlier than usual, excited to support his team. As he approached the stadium, he was surprised to find the area unusually quiet. After checking his ticket, he realized he was at the wrong stadium and quickly set off for the right one. On his way, he saw Mary, his old teacher, carrying a heavy bag by the side of the road. Tom hesitated, unsure whether to help her or continue to the game. Finally, he chose to stop and offer Mary a ride home, which she gladly accepted. When Tom helped her carry the bag into her apartment, he discovered that her living room window overlooked the correct stadium. They could still see the game, so they watched it together from Mary's living room.

"四要素"笔记法实战：

人物：Tom(T), friend(fr), Mary(M, teacher)
动词：want→got ticket→left→found quiet→wrong stadium→set off
     →saw M(w/ heavy bag)→hesitated→gave ride→watched together
主线：Tom got ticket → went to wrong stadium → met teacher Mary
     → helped her → discovered her window faced correct stadium → watched game together
情感：↑happy → !surprised(quiet) → ?hesitate → √helped → ↑happy(together)

复述框架（过去时、第三人称）：

开头：Tom was a big soccer fan and got a ticket to his favorite team's game.
发展：He left early but found the stadium strangely quiet — he was at the WRONG one.
转折：On the way to the right stadium, he met his old teacher Mary with a heavy bag.
高潮：He hesitated, then gave her a ride. At her home, he found her window faced the correct stadium.
结局：They watched the game together from her living room.

避坑：① 人称统一用 he / 过去时；② 抓"走错球场→遇老师→一起看"主线，不纠缠 decorative 细节；③ 卡壳用 "And then..." 缓冲，不停顿回头纠正。

三、真题母题·Part B（2024 广东听说 家乡变化访谈）

三问（考生英译中提问）：

  1. 那时候你的家乡是什么样的？→ What was your hometown like at that time?
  2. 这些变化是怎么发生的？→ How did the change happen?
  3. 他们如何增加收入？→ How do they increase their income?

五答（考生中译英回答，要点）：

  1. Q: 你的家乡在哪？A: In northern China.
  2. Q: 家乡什么时候开始闻名？A: In 2023.
  3. Q: 父亲小时候为什么怕沙尘暴？A: The village was buried by the sandstorm.
  4. Q: 为什么树不够大？A: Because the trees were not big enough.
  5. Q: uncle 以什么谋生？A: Making a living by producing and selling fruit juice.

策略：三问先把中文要点翻成简单英文问句（疑问词+助动词+主语+谓语），避免复杂从句；五答抓"数字/地点/原因"三类高频信息，用完整简单句。

四、真题母题·Part A（2024 广东听说 海洋潮汐能）

主题：海洋、潮汐能水力发电的优势、不足和保护措施（无生词，连读多）。 模仿要点：① 长句按意群停顿（主语后 / 从句前）；② 易连读处（tidal energy → /daɪdl ˈenədʒi/）提前慢读；③ 段尾降调表陈述结束。

▶ 第1题详解（Part C 四要素笔记法）

参考笔记：

人物：Tom(T), friend(fr), Mary(M, old teacher) 动词链：got ticket → left early → found quiet → wrong stadium → set off → saw M(w/ heavy bag) → hesitated → gave ride → watched together 主线：T got ticket → wrong stadium → met Mary → helped her → window faced correct stadium → watched together 情感：↑happy → !surprised(quiet) → ?hesitate → √helped → ↑happy(together)

易错提醒：第一遍听只抓核心动词链和人物；情感标注用 ↑/↓/!/? 符号，省书写时间；复述时统一用 he + 过去时。

▶ 第2题详解（Part B 三问速构）

(1) What was your hometown like at that time? (2) How did the change happen? / How did these changes take place? (3) How do they increase their income? / How do they make more money?

分析：

  • (1) "什么样"→ What...like；过去时（at that time）→ was。
  • (2) "怎么发生"→ How；过去时 → did。
  • (3) "如何增加"→ How；一般现在时 → do。

易错提醒：三问的中文提示逐词对照翻译；疑问词前置 + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词原形；时态看提示中的时间词。

▶ 第3题详解（Part A 意群停顿与语调）

参考标注：

Tidal energy is a clean and renewable source of power ↘. / It uses the movement / of ocean tides / to generate electricity ↘. / Although the technology is still developing ↗, / it holds great promise for the future ↘.

分析：

  • 介词短语前可停顿（of ocean tides / to generate electricity）。
  • 让步从句句中停顿用微升 ↗（Although...developing），主句句末降调 ↘。
  • 陈述句句末一律降调 ↘。

易错提醒：陈述句降调是铁律；从句在前、主句在后时，从句末尾用微升表"未说完"；连读处（tidal~energy）不切断意群。
